STRANGER THINGS

Set in the 1980s in the small town of Hawkins, Indiana, Stranger Things follows a group of kids who uncover a secret government experiment that opens a portal to a terrifying alternate dimension called the Upside Down. As they search for their missing friend, they encounter supernatural forces, a mysterious girl with psychic powers, and dark government secrets. The series blends nostalgic pop culture with suspense and emotional storytelling.

THE SUMMER I TURNED PRETTY

Based on the bestselling novel by Jenny Han, The Summer I Turned Pretty follows Belly, a teenage girl who returns to the beach house she visits every summer with her family and close friends. This summer, everything changes as she finds herself caught in a love triangle between two brothers, Conrad and Jeremiah. It's a heartfelt coming-of-age story about first love, heartbreak, and growing up.
